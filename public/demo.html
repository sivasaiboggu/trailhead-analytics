WALKING JOURNAL

The ridge walk

A morning route through the pines, with a lunch stop above the tree line.

Distance8.4 km
Ascent620 m
TimeAbout 3 hours
StartNorth gate, gravel path

Route notes

What should I pack?

Water, a windproof layer and a paper map for the ridge.

Where does the trail start?

Take the gravel path from the north gate to the first wooden bridge.

Where is the lunch stop?

On the flat rock above the tree line, just past the second cairn. There is shade from the pines until midday.

Packing checklist

  • Water, two litres
  • Windproof layer
  • Paper map of the ridge
  • Lunch and a snack
  • Small first-aid kit
